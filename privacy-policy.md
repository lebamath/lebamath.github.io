## 中文

本网站（www.wirelesssharenotes.com，以下称"本站"）尊重并保护访问者的隐私。本政策说明本站收集哪些信息，以及第三方广告服务如何使用 Cookie。

### 我们收集的信息

本站使用 **Google Analytics**（Google 统计分析工具）来了解访客如何使用本站，例如访问了哪些页面、停留时长、设备类型等，帮助我们改进内容。本站不会主动收集姓名、住址等个人身份信息。

### 第三方广告与 Cookie

本站使用 **Google AdSense** 展示广告。Google 作为第三方广告服务商，会在您访问本站及互联网上其他网站时使用 Cookie（包括但不限于 DoubleClick Cookie）来投放广告，广告投放会基于您以往对本站及其他网站的访问记录。

您可以通过以下方式管理个性化广告：

- 访问 [Google 广告设置](https://adssettings.google.com/) 停用个性化广告
- 访问 [www.aboutads.info](http://www.aboutads.info/choices/) 停用第三方供应商用于个性化广告的 Cookie

关于 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的数据，可以参见 [Google 合作伙伴的隐私和条款说明](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)。

### 儿童隐私

本站内容面向对无线通信技术感兴趣的一般公众，不专门面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 联系方式

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过邮件联系：[wirelessShareNotes@foxmail.com](mailto:wirelessShareNotes@foxmail.com)

## English

This website (www.wirelesssharenotes.com, "this site") respects and protects the privacy of its visitors. This policy explains what information we collect and how third-party advertising services use cookies.

### Information We Collect

This site uses **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are visited, time spent, and device type — in order to improve content. This site does not actively collect personally identifiable information such as names or addresses.

### Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

This site uses **Google AdSense** to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads on this site based on a user's prior visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You may manage personalized advertising by:

- Visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) to opt out of personalized advertising
- Visiting [www.aboutads.info](http://www.aboutads.info/choices/) to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising

For more information on how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Children's Privacy

This site's content is aimed at a general audience interested in wireless communication technology. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
